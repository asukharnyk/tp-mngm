Reading it — how the columns, bars and colours work
The first two columns stay put while you scroll the timeline sideways. Purple rows are Design System features, teal are features owned by other teams, collapsed by default with the same fields. Bars sit in the week they fall in; the amber column is the current week. Ticket bars sit inside their epic's window, packed back from the due date; done and design-review tickets get no bar. Red bars mark epics whose start has already passed — the due date is out of reach at that estimate. Epics are ordered by nearest due date. keep in DS marks an other-team epic that should stay with the design system because the platform consumes it; hand over marks one with a single consumer that belongs with the owning team. Hover either for the reason, and use Ownership to show only one set. The reasoning is in the Summary tab. Blocker chips are links — each shows its own due date, cleared ones are struck through. Exp. start counts backwards from the Jira due date: an epic needing 6 days and due 16 Sep starts 9 Sep (weekends skipped). Min / Mid / Max redraw everything. Untick a column to drop it; Timeline off makes the table fill the width. Notes live only while this view is open — use Copy notes to keep them.
